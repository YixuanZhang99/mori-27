# OpenEvolve continuation under one cumulative budget

The first real pair used source `49fb076`, whose frozen gate cannot admit
later seeds. Its reference trace also lacks complete per-iteration coverage.
Do not modify that source, replace its scores, retry either claimed cell, or
represent later results from another source as the remaining cells of the
same frozen experiment. The first pair and its partial audit remain available.

To continue C05, add a new, explicitly versioned experiment within the
**same** original campaign journal and its existing ¥200 ceiling. A new
experiment is not a fresh budget and cannot erase previous costs, reservations,
pending calls, claims or proofs. Its append-only binding must name a unique
experiment ID, pinned source tree and runtime, complete plan and protocol,
the original campaign and prior journal head, the first-pair evidence identity,
and a human-readable list of method changes. Cell and run identities must be
unambiguous across experiments, and all model reservations and settlements
must use the original campaign's atomic accounting and global available funds.

Migration may be admitted only against an independently pinned current head
when the campaign has no unresolved provider operation, outstanding protected
capacity or budget breach. Read and check those conditions, compare the exact
head and append the unique experiment binding in one ledger write transaction;
a separate preflight cannot authorize a later write. Persist and synchronize the
source, runtime and manifests and verify their identities before binding; no
paid work is allowed until both original bindings are durable. Interrupted
pre-binding preparation must be recoverable without replacing artifacts, while
an interrupted post-binding step may resume only the same original identity.
It must preserve access to every historical pilot,
runtime, clock, worker claim, run binding, evidence anchor and first-pair audit
under its original identity. Readers of old journals must retain their original
meaning; a new event must never reinterpret old cells as new ones. Any change
to the journal format must be documented with its backward-read limitations.

The new version must fix the unconditional later-seed gate: its first matched
pair may run only after its own frozen source/runtime, global currency,
supervision and isolation preflight pass. Later seeds remain denied until the
new first pair has a verified complete accounting, geometry and replay decision.
Every new cell retains an original nonextendable deadline, whole-cell process
containment, a single bound run, frozen provider/price parameters and declared
per-cell limits. Recovery must never replace its original clock, process or
currency identity or retry an uncertain paid operation.
The verifier must distinguish a settled, measured failed model response from
unknown usage or a lost broker: only explicitly settled outcomes may proceed
under the existing attempt and total-budget bounds, and an unknown, unresolved
or late response must stop without retry. Preserve all failed attempts and
their usage. The reference trace must bind submitted iterations, prompts,
model receipts, patch outcomes, stages, admission/migration transitions and
final selection sufficiently to determine complete coverage, including known
failures; a host-observed event alone is not proof of the entire database state.

Report the old pair and new experiment separately, including source hashes,
protocol differences, every seed and arm, recorded cost upper bounds, pending
and reserved amounts, invoice verification status, and unresolved or unrun
cells. A separately obtained invoice must identify its source. Only combine
numbers across versions after an explicit, tested
equivalence argument; do not describe them as a single fixed-source matrix by
default. The six-cell report and partial first-pair audit cannot open a gate.

Acceptance must cover old-journal replay, immutable history, duplicate or stale
experiment IDs and heads, concurrent reservations across experiments, the
global currency ceiling, interrupted migration and source/runtime binding,
unresolved calls, crashes and clock recovery. Exercise both genuine settled
failure and unknown provider outcomes under a fake transport, prove no retry
after uncertainty, and verify the complete-coverage gate before any paid seed
after the new first pair. Independent no-model, no-candidate replay must
reproduce the old pair and the new experiment's evidence without reading or
copying credentials.

## Candidate provider protocol for a future experiment

The frozen first pair used `reasoning_effort=low`; all six Mori completions
spent their output allowance on reasoning and produced no usable candidate.
A later experiment may explicitly set `reasoning_effort=none` and
`thinking.type=disabled` in both arms. DeepSeek's
[Chat Completions API](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/api/create-chat-completion/)
documents this combination. Freeze the changed setting in the new experiment,
provider binding, request receipts and report; it is a different protocol, not
an extension of the original fixed-source matrix. This is a testable hypothesis,
not evidence of improved performance. It does not authorize paid calls.
